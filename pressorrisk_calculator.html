PressorRisk

Pre-operative probability of escalation to a norepinephrine infusion after first-line bolus vasopressors fail, in non-cardiac surgery under general anaesthesia.

Research tool, not for clinical use. Derived on a single Korean tertiary centre (INSPIRE, 99,357 operations, 1,007 events) and never externally validated. Calibration drifts between surgical services, so another hospital would need to recalibrate both intercept and slope before these probabilities meant anything locally. Against a regression given the same procedure information this model gains only 0.007 of AUC: its purpose is a calibrated number, not better judgement than yours.

How the two numbers differ

The predicted risk is the full model: penalised logistic regression, 105 procedure groups, restricted cubic splines on age and BMI, cross-validated AUC 0.870 with a calibration slope of 1.00. The point score is a hand-computable simplification using five variables and reaching AUC 0.850. Where they disagree, the full model is the better estimate; the score exists for situations without a computer, and on decision-curve analysis it does not outperform a regression given the same procedure detail.

Derived from INSPIRE v1.4.2 (PhysioNet, doi 10.13026/1eay-yc85), Seoul National University Hospital, 2011 to 2020. Outcome: norepinephrine infusion started after a prior ephedrine or phenylephrine bolus. The age and BMI contributions are the lookup tables exported by the fitted model itself, so this page cannot extrapolate differently from the model, and inputs are restricted to the range the model has actually seen (age 20 to 90, BMI 10.2 to 66.7). It reproduces the model's own exported worked example exactly. No patient data is contained in or transmitted by this page; all computation happens in your browser.
